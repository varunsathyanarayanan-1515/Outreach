# Dream 100 Accounts: Accounting-Firm AI Implementation

**Built:** 2026-08-07  
**Discovery tools:** Exa and Firecrawl  
**Market:** US accounting, CPA, CAS, outsourced accounting, bookkeeping, controller, and fractional-CFO firms  
**Objective:** Rank the 100 accounts most likely to produce a commercially valuable lighthouse case study for the current offer.

## Method

Exa was used for semantic company discovery, firmographic data, employee growth, services, and hiring signals. Firecrawl was used to discover and validate company service pages, careers pages, software-customer stories, and workflow evidence.

The highest-quality source set was vendor customer stories from Karbon, Canopy, and Financial Cents. These firms are attractive because they:

- Already purchase operational technology
- Publicly acknowledge workflow or capacity problems
- Have demonstrated willingness to participate in a case study
- Often run recurring bookkeeping, CAS, or month-end-close workflows

### Scoring

| Category | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Recurring CAS/bookkeeping/controller/CFO fit | 25 |
| Firm size and implementation budget fit | 20 |
| Growth, hiring, client-volume, or capacity trigger | 20 |
| Technology and workflow signal | 20 |
| Lighthouse case-study value | 15 |

### Tiers

- **Tier A — ranks 1-20:** Begin high-touch Dream 100 work here. Firmographic or operational evidence is strongest.
- **Tier B — ranks 21-50:** Strong service and technology fit; verify remaining firmographics while Tier A is in production.
- **Tier C — ranks 51-100:** Valid service fit, but size, urgency, or exact domain may require another verification pass. The final four are intentionally larger strategic lighthouse accounts with longer sales cycles.

`Needs confirmation` means the source supports the company and signal, but the independent company domain or current employee count should be checked before outreach.

## Recommended first batch

Start with ranks **1-12**, not all 20 simultaneously. They have the clearest combination of:

- Relevant size
- Recurring workflow volume
- Current growth or hiring pressure
- Named operational leaders or accessible founders
- Existing comfort with accounting technology
- A public track record of discussing operational improvement

Create three deliverable variants:

1. **Capacity and hiring:** Full Send Finance, Main Accounting Services, Ascend, Attracct, A Strong CPA, abip, Walker Glantz.
2. **Multi-office orchestration:** Cascade CPA, TDT, JTC CPAs.
3. **Tech-stack and exception workflow:** kept.pro, Papin CPA, Cloud5, Basis 365, mAccounting.

## Required pre-outreach validation

Before sending a Dream 100 deliverable:

1. Confirm the current independent domain and that the firm is still operating under the listed brand.
2. Check current employee count and exclude firms below five people unless the opportunity is unusually strong.
3. Identify the managing partner plus the operations or CAS leader.
4. Confirm that the cited vendor story is still relevant to the current organization.
5. Search for a current trigger: hiring, new office, acquisition, new service line, migration, partner content, or recent growth.
6. Avoid assuming that use of Canopy, Karbon, or Financial Cents means the firm is dissatisfied. Position custom work as the layer between tools, not an automatic replacement.

## Important limitation

This is an evidence-backed account-discovery and ranking list, not a contact-ready database. Exa and Firecrawl support the company, service, and signal research, but roughly one-third of Tier B/C domains or current employee counts still require a final account-level check. Contact sourcing and email validation should be performed only after the first 20 accounts are approved for outreach.
