**Target Audience:** Mid-sized CPA and accounting firms (5-25 staff, 100+ client engagements) suffering from capacity constraints, stretched thin by month-end close, and stuck doing manual Excel work.

**Core Message / Positioning:**
*"Stop paying accountants to be data entry clerks. We orchestrate and automate your existing tools so your team can focus on high-margin advisory work. AI doesn't replace your accountants; it elevates them from doers to reviewers."*

*The "Process Doctor" entry point.*

**Price:** 
- Initial Strategy (To build case studies): Free or heavily discounted

- **Customized Implementation Roadmap:** A step-by-step blueprint to achieve Level 2 AI orchestration.

**The Pitch:**
*"We’ll come in and map out exactly where your firm is losing time to manual work and where AI can be injected for immediate ROI—like cutting month-end close by 7 days. We hand you the blueprint. If you want us to build it, we can. If you want to take it and implement it yourself, it's yours to keep."*

## 2. High-Ticket Offer: The Custom "AI-Native Firm" Build-Out
*Full-scale workflow orchestration. We sell outcomes and capacity expansion, not hours.*

**Pricing Benchmarks:**
- **Single Workflow Automation (e.g., Document Processing Pipeline):** $25,000 – $65,000 (4-6 weeks)
- **Multi-Workflow Implementation (Back-Office Suite):** $75,000 – $120,000 (6-8 weeks)
- **Full Practice Automation Package:** $120,000 – $180,000+ (10-14 weeks)
- **Retained Advisory/Maintenance:** $3,000 – $8,000/mo

**Core Packages:**

### A. The "Close & Collect" Pipeline (Fastest ROI)
- **Document Intake & Processing:** AI OCR classification for invoices, receipts, bank statements (70% time reduction).
- **Client Onboarding & Chasing:** Automated systems for chasing down missing documents and W-9s via email/SMS. Saves 10+ hours a week and shrinks onboarding from weeks to days.

### B. Month-End Close & Bookkeeping Orchestrator
- **Transactions-to-Trial-Balance:** Implementing agents that ingest bank feeds, code transactions against the firm's specific chart of accounts, and surface only the exceptions for human review.
- **Outcome:** Reduces a 10-12 day month-end close to 2-3 days, expanding firm capacity by up to 55%.

### C. Partner & Practice Multiplier (Sales & Knowledge CRM)
- **Custom CRM & Sales Pipeline Optimization:** Automating lead tracking, AI-generated proposals, engagement letters, and unified billing.
- **Firm Knowledge RAG:** Encoding the firm's historical workpapers and rules into an internal RAG agent, cutting the 1-5 hours daily wasted on information search.

### D. Tech Stack Consolidation & SaaS Replacement (The "Stop Renting" Angle)
- **What it is:** Replacing expensive, bloated off-the-shelf software (where they only use 20% of the features) with a bespoke, AI-native internal tool.
- **Outcome:** Substantially lower Total Cost of Ownership (TCO). They stop paying huge per-seat licenses for generic software and instead own a tool that fits their exact workflow.

**The Pitch:**
*"We don't just sell you another SaaS tool; we replace the ones you're overpaying for. We encode your firm's specific expertise into an orchestration layer that fits your exact workflow. Your accountants will log in and see a dashboard of 'exceptions to review' rather than 'lines of data to enter.' By automating document processing, month-end close, and replacing your bloated tech stack, we unlock the capacity for you to scale high-margin Advisory services—all while lowering your software overhead."*
